Monthly avg talk time here is weighted by answered calls (days you answered more count more), so it can differ slightly from the My Performance card, which uses the same simple average your manager's table shows. Delta chips compare each month to the one before it.
What do these numbers mean?
Answered / Missed
Calls that rang you during the work window and were answered or missed by you. A missed ring may still have been answered by a teammate afterward.
Answer rate
Answered ÷ (Answered + Missed). The company display standard is 92%.
Avg talk time
The average length of your answered calls — the same figure your manager's table shows.
"Waited"
How long the caller had been on the line (from phone-system pickup, including the menu) when that ring reached you. Shown only for calls the call-capture system has; older rings show the time alone.
Team average
Your whole department's roster, averaged. Individual teammates are never shown here.
